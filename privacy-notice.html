Who is responsible for your information?

[Facility legal name and contact details to be completed by the facility.] The facility is responsible for deciding how information in this system is used and protected.

Information used for care and operations

The system may contain identity and contact details, health records, appointments, prescriptions, diagnostic results, billing information, consent records, and access history. Staff access is intended to support care, administration, safety, and approved facility operations.

Sharing

Information may be shared with care team members and service providers for purposes authorized by the facility. The facility must document the specific purposes, recipients, safeguards, and any required authorization before sharing.

Your choices and requests

You may contact the facility to ask about access, correction, or restriction of information, or to raise a privacy concern. The facility will assess each request and explain any limits that apply.

Retention and security

The facility sets retention periods and access controls. Clinical information may be retained where needed for care, safety, operational, or other facility-approved purposes. A request to erase information is not an instruction to remove clinical records.
