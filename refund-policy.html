Refund Policy

This policy describes the refund request and approval process currently supported by OneMed. The facility that provided care handles requests through its billing team.

Request and review

  • Ask the facility’s billing team to submit a request identifying the payment, amount, and reason.
  • Accounts reviews each request independently. The staff member who submitted a request cannot approve it.
  • A request cannot exceed the remaining amount of the posted payment after other pending or approved refunds are accounted for.
  • Accounts may approve or reject a request. An approved request records an accounting reversal and restores the corresponding invoice balance.

How the refund is returned

An accounting approval records the reversal in OneMed; it does not itself send money back through a payment provider or confirm that funds have reached you. Contact the facility’s billing team for the status of any payment return.

Terms the facility must confirm: OneMed does not currently define refund eligibility grounds, a request deadline, processing time, fees, or the return method. Ask the facility’s billing team for those terms before relying on them. This page will need updating when the facility adopts them.

For help with a request, contact the billing team at the facility where the payment was made.
