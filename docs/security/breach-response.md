# Privacy incident and breach response

**Working draft for facility review. This is an operational template, not legal advice and not a statement of legal compliance.** The facility's privacy officer and legal counsel must approve the process and confirm applicable notification duties before use.

## Immediate response

1. Report a suspected loss, disclosure, alteration, or unauthorized access to the facility privacy officer and security contact as soon as discovered.
2. Preserve relevant system, access, audit, message, and physical records. Do not alter or delete evidence.
3. Contain ongoing access or disclosure where this can be done safely. Record each action, time, decision maker, and operational impact.
4. Record the incident in the restricted breach register, including discovery time, affected data categories, approximate number of people, known recipients, containment steps, and open questions.
5. Assess scope and likely effects with clinical, security, operational, privacy, and legal leads. Document facts separately from assumptions.
6. Have counsel and the privacy officer decide whether affected people, regulators, or other parties must be notified, by whom, and through which approved channel. Record the decision and its basis even when no notification is made.
7. Track remediation, communication, review, and closure actions to named owners and dates.

## Review and closure

The privacy officer should confirm that containment and notification decisions are documented, evidence is preserved, affected records are protected, corrective actions have owners, and any legal hold remains active where required. Closure should include a dated review and an accountable approver.
