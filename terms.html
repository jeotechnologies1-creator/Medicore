Draft only. This text is a working template. It has not been reviewed by the facility's lawyer and does not claim legal compliance. The facility must replace or approve it before relying on it.

Access to the service

Use OneMed only through an account issued or enabled by the facility. Keep authentication details private and report suspected account misuse promptly.

Accurate use

Staff must use the system only for authorized work and document care and administrative activity accurately. Patients should use the portal for their own account and contact the facility when information appears incorrect.

Availability and support

The facility provides local instructions for urgent care, service outages, and support. Do not rely on the portal for emergency communication.
